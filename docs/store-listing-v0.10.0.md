# Ficha de Microsoft Store — Luma Profiles 0.10.0

Paquete: `dist/store/LumaProfiles.msixupload` (generado con `scripts/build-msix.ps1`, versión de paquete `0.10.0.0`).
La descripción, el subtítulo y las palabras clave no cambian respecto a `store-listing-v0.9.0.md`, salvo la cifra de perfiles: **77** en lugar de 67. Conviene renovar la captura 1 (biblioteca), que ahora muestra la fila «¿Qué vas a hacer?».
El aviso de nueva versión no existe en la versión de la Store (la Store la actualiza sola), así que «sin conexión a Internet» sigue siendo cierto en la ficha.

El campo «Novedades de esta versión» admite 1.500 caracteres: el texto acumula todas las versiones publicadas en la Store, la más reciente primero (ES 1316 · EN 1289 · PT 1268 caracteres).


## English (United States) — What's new in this version

```
0.10.0
- "What are you going to do?": Play, Movies and series, Work, Read, At night and Photo and design narrow the library in one click.
- Recent: your last 6 profiles, one click away.
- 10 new profiles (77): Battery saver, Night coding, Dark room, Web sRGB, Video call, Series at night, Social media, MOBA, Retro and Fighting.
- A different profile on each display; schedule, rules and shortcuts pick displays.
- Game mode: automatic profile for any full-screen app.
- Battery profile: switches when the laptop is unplugged and back when plugged in.
- Undo/redo adjustments (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
- HDR: detects HDR displays and keeps their gamma neutral.

0.9.0
- 7 game profiles: Military and tactical shooter, Battle royale, Horror, Open world, Racing and Cartoon.
- Apply without saving: try values without changing the profile.

0.8.1 – 0.8.3
- 17 accent colors and "Your color".
- Time-slot schedule, sunrise and sunset, with smooth transitions.
- Ctrl+Alt shortcuts per profile and your own profiles.
- Export/import profiles, schedule, rules and shortcuts.
- Tray and start with Windows; 30 s confirmation for shortcuts too.

0.8.0
- 10 modes for VA displays and a restore point.
- Before/after preview and 30 s confirmation.
- Search with filters, 5 views, themes and multi-monitor.
```
